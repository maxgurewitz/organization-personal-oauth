Privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026. This policy describes Organization Personal, a personal-use Gmail and Google Calendar integration maintained by Maxwell Gurewitz. The integration is under development and account authorization has not yet been activated.

Information accessed and purpose

With the owner's Google authorization, the application accesses account identity, email messages and metadata, calendar lists and events as needed for the owner's requested productivity tasks. Additional permissions support saving Gmail drafts, sending messages, and creating, updating or deleting owned calendar events. It requests only permissions needed for enabled features.

Use and sharing

Information is used to answer the owner's requests and prepare or execute their approved actions. Relevant content may be provided to the application's AI provider, OpenAI, to process those requests. Approved messages or calendar invitations disclose the approved content to the chosen recipients through Google. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this application to train general-purpose AI models. Before activation, any processing providers must be configured consistently with Google's applicable data-use requirements.

Organization Personal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, security and retention

OAuth credentials, retained task content and action records are private application data, not public website content. The planned service retains credentials while the account is connected and retains proposals and action records to support review and reliable execution. Deployment must protect credentials from agent access and enforce direct owner approval for Google writes before write features are activated. This website does not claim that the unfinished service is already deployed or isolated.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke Google access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase previously retained application records. As operator, the owner can remove stored credentials and retained application data; deletion controls must be available before activation.

This public website

These are static informational pages hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics, forms, advertising or application cookies. GitHub may process ordinary request information to provide hosting, under its privacy statement. Do not submit Google data or credentials to the public repository.

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy can be raised in the documentation issue tracker without including private information. This policy will be updated before material changes to data handling.
