Organization Personal

Organization Personal is a personal-use application maintained by Maxwell Gurewitz to work with his own Gmail and Google Calendar account. It is not offered for public registration.

What the application does

The integration is being developed to search and read email and calendar information, prepare local email and event proposals, and carry out approved actions. Saving a local proposal does not send an email or change Google Calendar. Saving a Gmail draft, sending mail, and creating, changing or deleting calendar events are separate actions requiring owner approval.

Google account access

Access is granted through Google's OAuth consent screen for the specific permissions requested. This informational website does not sign you in, receive OAuth callbacks, or collect Gmail or Calendar data. Account authorization and the protected execution service are still being set up.

Support

This is an owner-maintained personal application. For questions about this public documentation, use the documentation issue tracker. Do not post private messages, calendar details, passwords or tokens there.
